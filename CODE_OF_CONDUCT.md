# Code of conduct

Knode follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work.
Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are not tolerated in any Knode space:
issues, pull requests, discussions, chats or events.

To report a problem, write to **oss@knode.md**. Reports are handled privately, and
maintainers will act on them as the Covenant describes, up to a permanent ban.
